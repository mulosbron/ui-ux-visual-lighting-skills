# Premium Typography Brand Kit (Mobile & Landing Page)

Use this skill as the canonical reference for pairing specific premium display fonts with optimal secondary (body) fonts to establish high-end, editorial, and utilitarian minimalist interfaces. 

## Core Typographic Architecture
To establish an editorial and premium feel, this kit strictly follows the principle of typographic contrast: high-impact display fonts for headings (`H1`, `H2`) combined with highly legible, clean sans-serif or serif fonts for body text and UI elements.

> **CRITICAL RULE:** Do NOT use generic default fonts like "Inter", "Roboto", or "Open Sans" for body text. Use refined alternatives like "Geist Sans", "SF Pro Display", "Satoshi", or "Switzer".

---

## 1. Behind Nineties (Retro / 90's Vibe)
* **Source:** Envato Elements
* **Characteristics:** A bold, eye-catching retro display font invoking 90s nostalgia. Excellent for high-energy, Gen-Z targeted, or brutalist projects.
* **Optimal Secondary Font:** **Geist Sans** or **Space Grotesk**
* **Why:** Behind Nineties has a dominant character. Pairing it with the neutral yet slightly technical Geist Sans or Space Grotesk ensures excellent legibility in UI without competing with the heading's loud aesthetic.

## 2. Brand (Modern & Aesthetic Display)
* **Source:** yfonts.xyz
* **Characteristics:** Minimalist, elegant, and focused on "premium" aesthetics. Ideal for fashion, luxury consumption, or high-end portfolio landing pages.
* **Optimal Secondary Font:** **Satoshi** (Fontshare) or **Switzer**
* **Why:** Satoshi provides a modern, clean structure that complements the elegance of Brand without breaking the minimalist harmony.

## 3. GC Epic Pro (Wide & Futuristic)
* **Source:** Envato Elements
* **Characteristics:** A "Future Extended Sans". It is geometric, wide (extended), and futuristic. Highly suitable for tech, SaaS startups, and modern mobile UI headings.
* **Optimal Secondary Font:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** or **Geist Sans**
* **Why:** To support Epic Pro's wide geometric structure while maintaining readability at small mobile sizes, a modern neo-grotesque like Plus Jakarta Sans perfectly bridges the futuristic vibe with mobile utility.

## 4. Extenda Family (Dynamic & Variable)
* **Source:** Zetafonts
* **Characteristics:** A highly versatile variable sans-serif family ranging from ultra-condensed to extended. Creates a powerful and dynamic brand identity.
* **Optimal Secondary Font:** **SF Pro Display / Text** (Apple Native) or **Helvetica Neue**
* **Why:** Because Extenda introduces significant typographic tension and dynamism, the body font must be completely "invisible" and platform-native to prevent UI fatigue, especially in mobile applications.

## 5. Nova Klasse (Geometric & Playful)
* **Source:** Envato Elements
* **Characteristics:** A variable sans-serif blending circular/rectangular forms with organic details. It feels professional yet playful, perfect for fintech or edtech apps.
* **Optimal Secondary Font:** **Outfit** or **Instrument Serif** / **Newsreader**
* **Why:** Use Outfit for a harmonious geometric sans-serif pairing. If aiming for an editorial/premium contrast, an elegant serif like Instrument Serif or Newsreader creates a sophisticated juxtaposition.

---

## General Execution Directives

1. **Hierarchy & Application:** Apply premium display fonts EXCLUSIVELY to `H1`, `H2`, and major hero text. All mobile UI elements (buttons, descriptions, long paragraphs, forms) MUST use the secondary body font.
2. **Color & Contrast:** Body text must never be absolute black (`#000000`). Use off-black (`#111111` or `#2F3437`). Ensure high contrast between the background and premium display fonts to highlight their character.
3. **Spacing (Tracking & Leading):** 
   - Tighten the tracking (`letter-spacing`) slightly on extended fonts like Epic Pro and Extenda.
   - Maintain a generous line-height (`1.5` to `1.6`) for all secondary body fonts to maximize mobile scannability.
4. **Mobile Feasibility:** Remember that mobile is touch-first. Secondary fonts must remain strictly legible at `14px` - `16px` with adequate touch targets (minimum `44pt`/`48dp`).
